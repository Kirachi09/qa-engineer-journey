#  What is Software Testing?

## Definition

## Definition

Software testing is the process of evaluating a software system to verify that it meets its specified requirements and produces the expected results while identifying defects before release.

---

## Key Points

- Ensures the software meets business and user requirements.
- Helps identify defects before software reaches users.
- Improves software quality, reliability, and user satisfaction.

---

## Real Example

## Real Example

During my manual testing of the Expense Diary application, I performed black-box testing to verify whether the system behaved according to its requirements. One issue I discovered was that the application accepted "NaN" as an expense value, even though expense amounts should always be valid numeric values.

## Reflection

### What I Learned

Software testing helps detect defects early in the development process, reducing the chances of issues reaching production.

### What Surprised Me

I was surprised to learn that many small to medium-sized software teams do not have dedicated QA engineers. Instead, developers often perform basic testing themselves.

### My Question

How does the QA team collaborate with developers to ensure software quality throughout the development lifecycle?
