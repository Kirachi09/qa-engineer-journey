# V-Model in Software Testing

## Definition

The V-Model is a verification and validation model where development activities are represented on the left side of the V and corresponding testing activities are represented on the right side. It emphasizes testing alongside each development phase to detect defects earlier.

---

## Key Points

- Testing activities are planned alongside development activities.
- Each development phase has a corresponding testing phase.
- It emphasizes early testing, documentation, and traceability.
- It can be applied across different phases of the SDLC.
